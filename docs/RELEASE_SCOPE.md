# Research and distribution scope

The current manuscript reports a source-bound retrospective 2023 analysis of two Himalayan query ROIs. Authentic Sentinel-2 observations span January–November, with a shared September Sentinel-1 acquisition. The expanded source manifest, fitted-state hashes, scores, tables and figures identify this analysis separately from preserved earlier diagnostic packets.

The scientific contribution concerns dependency-safe masked reconstruction and descriptive surface-anomaly trajectories. It does not establish calibrated event probabilities, causal precursors, prospective warning lead time, annual false-alarm rates, regional generalization, or civil-defense deployment. Missing independent labels and verified exposure cannot be repaired by assigning event labels to every pre/post image. Seeds measure optimization variability conditional on the same development corpus.

Fitting uses the declared pre-breach period and parity-0 pixels; parity-1 development monitoring shares acquisition scenes. Earlier pre-period trajectories are development-exposed retrospective reconstructions even though their input contexts exclude later acquisitions. The source grids are query-centroid ROIs rather than validated shorelines. SAR ellipsoid tiepoints do not provide DEM terrain correction. Candidate spectral masks and signed near-zero-denominator indices require interpretation within their actual QA support.

Earlier fixture-derived or invalidly fitted outputs remain quarantined. The new analysis does not rehabilitate their numerical claims. Poor, failed or inconclusive results in the current analysis must remain visible.

A local compiled manuscript is a reviewable artifact. It does not constitute external submission, peer review, or publication. Original repository history, previous failure evidence, and dated export bundles remain preserved. No new external publication, Git push, or release licensing is authorized by artifact preparation.

The root license remains authoritative. Provider observations retain their source terms and attribution requirements. Credential values are never part of the public artifact set. A portable installation and independent external replication remain separate tasks; local tests do not establish them.
