\begin{abstract}
Retrospective multisensor anomaly modeling requires a defensible separation between authentic observations, engineering diagnostics and independently validated warning performance. We assemble a source-bound diagnostic workflow for South Lhonak and Khangchung Tsho using one shared September 2023 Sentinel-1 product and two distinct Sentinel-2 products. Four site--sensor groups retain asynchronous acquisition support, explicit missingness and source hashes. Native SAR calibration is verified numerically; target-grid SAR remains an ellipsoid/tiepoint diagnostic without terrain correction or validated lake registration. A masked autoencoder with 1,062,407 parameters is optimized in five local diagnostic runs using frozen training-only normalization and dependency-safe optical masking. Matched score records, genuine checkpoint attention distributions and preserved null results are reported. The monitor masked reconstruction errors range from 0.00510621716135 to 0.0105662554769 on shared, development-exposed support; these values do not estimate population performance. RPCA remains nonconverged. Independent labels, threshold-calibration support, eligible exposure and an adequate temporal corpus are absent, so precision--recall, annual false-alarm trade-offs and warning lead time remain unavailable. The contribution of this draft is an auditable diagnostic record and a precise account of the remaining scientific dependencies, not a validated GLOF forecast or operational alert system.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Glacial-lake monitoring joins a physical observation problem with a statistical decision problem. Authentic image values alone do not establish lake-specific change, independent anomaly labels or a prospective warning threshold. The retained source register includes regional glacier and lake-risk studies~\cite{LIT001,LIT004,LIT005,LIT006}, retrospective GLOF detection and inventory studies~\cite{LIT002,LIT003}, and event-specific case material~\cite{LIT010,LIT011,LIT012}. These references motivate source and task differentiation; their numerical findings are not transferred into this diagnostic dataset.

Sentinel-GL asks whether a temporal masked autoencoder can eventually provide useful information beyond classical, seasonal, physical and observation-quality alternatives. That question is not answered by the present pilot. The complete intended program still requires independent measurement and event ascertainment, fitted embedding-reference scoring, a frozen score combination, fair baselines, controlled perturbations, factorial and sensitivity analyses, out-of-distribution tests, independent evaluation and reproducibility. Negative and inconclusive outcomes remain admissible.

The present paper-style draft reports the completed engineering and bounded optimization diagnostics. It neither substitutes those diagnostics for missing research evidence nor reduces the intended study because of venue or timing.

\section{Data and Observation Support}
\subsection{Products, sites and geometry}
The pilot query centroids are South Lhonak (27.915$^\circ$N, 88.204$^\circ$E) and Khangchung Tsho (27.985$^\circ$N, 88.752$^\circ$E). These are query locations, not independently validated lake boundaries. Each target grid is $499\times499$ cells in EPSG:32645 with 20-m spacing; this spacing is not a claim of 20-m sensor resolution or terrain-corrected geometric accuracy.

Four site--sensor records originate from three unique provider products. Both SAR records use the same S1A IW GRD VV/VH acquisition dated 1 September 2023. Optical observations are dated 4 September for South Lhonak and 21 September for Khangchung Tsho. The retained SAR start/stop strings do not explicitly specify a timezone. Historical 2023 provider availability is unknown, and later processing cannot establish operational availability in 2023. Figure~\ref{fig:obs} shows the actual sparse support rather than an interpolated annual sequence.

\subsection{Optical validity and missingness}
B03, B08 and B11 retain source-derived reflectance, including valid negative values where present. The indices are
\begin{align}
\mathrm{NDWI}&=\frac{B03-B08}{B03+B08},\\
\mathrm{MNDWI}&=\frac{B03-B11}{B03+B11},
\end{align}
with invalid or unsupported denominators represented explicitly. MNDWI is not duplicated as a separate NDSI measurement. The source grids contain 249,001 cells each; SCL classes 4/5/6 cover 104,366 South Lhonak cells and 9,286 Khangchung cells. Finite observed values and clear-class eligibility are distinct masks, not interchangeable claims of usable lake coverage. Weather, DEM, coherence and unstaged optical bands remain unavailable.

\subsection{SAR radiometry and spatial limits}
For raw digital number $D$, calibration factor $A_\sigma$ and supported range/azimuth noise terms, the implemented numerical transform is
\begin{align}
\sigma^0_{\rm raw}&=D^2/A_\sigma^2,\\
\sigma^0_{\rm signed}&=(D^2-N_{\rm range}N_{\rm azimuth})/A_\sigma^2.
\end{align}
The product-specific LUTs, flags, image coordinates and block support govern application. Signed noise-subtracted values remain signed in linear diagnostics; $10\log_{10}(\sigma^0)$ is defined only on positive finite support. No arbitrary dB floor restores nonpositive power. Sixteen actual source-sample arithmetic checks were retained.

The plotted and panel SAR channels are \emph{raw calibrated} $\sigma^0$ target-grid diagnostics, not the native signed-denoised variant. Native calibration does not establish terrain correction. Annotation tiepoint inversion and linear-power resampling produce an ellipsoid-only diagnostic; DEM-based terrain correction, layover/shadow QA, independently validated lake geometry and cross-sensor registration uncertainty remain unavailable. The distributions in Figure~\ref{fig:sar} are spatial diagnostics, not temporal anomaly traces or water-only measurements.

\section{Diagnostic Modeling and Inference}
\subsection{Asynchronous panel and target masking}
The source-bound panel stores optical and SAR acquisition groups separately, retaining channel units, masks, hashes and support. There is no temporal interpolation or forward fill. Computational padding is not an observation and cannot become a loss target.

Optical indices are deterministic functions of source bands. Hiding B03 while leaving B08 and NDWI visible would leak the target through $B03=B08(1+\mathrm{NDWI})/(1-\mathrm{NDWI})$ on its defined domain. The accepted dependency-closure policy therefore hides all observed optical channels at a targeted coordinate from encoder context while preserving exactly the requested genuine-valid loss targets. Dependency-hidden context, natural missingness, artificial targets and padding remain distinct.

\subsection{Architecture, fitting and monitoring}
The actual model has latent width 128, four attention heads, four encoder layers, two decoder layers and feed-forward widths 512/256. Its measured parameter count is 1,062,407, superseding an earlier approximate 401K estimate. Pre-layer normalization and final normalization are recorded. Explicit UTC optical time features are encoded; unresolved SAR times remain unencoded. Bounded source-derived quadrant summaries avoid unbounded pixel-wise attention and do not create new independent samples.

Training and monitoring use complementary 16-pixel checkerboard-tile parity on the same exposed scenes. A transform is fitted only on declared parity-0 valid context and frozen for parity-1 application, checkpoint replay and extensions. This is not independent lake/event validation. Five seeds (0--4) run under a prospectively recorded configuration after a four-epoch learning pilot. Main runs lasted 12, 12, 12, 12 and 11 epochs; checkpoint eligibility began at epoch 6, and selected epochs were 10, 10, 12, 11 and 8. Three-epoch extensions and their adverse/mixed monitor outcomes remain retained. Neither short execution nor checkpoint replay proves convergence. The training runner's OS exit code was not captured; its completed artifacts, state updates and replay checks are available, while this telemetry limitation remains explicit.

\subsection{Score definitions and baselines}
Score A is observed hidden-target squared error divided by its actual target count:
\begin{equation}
A=\frac{\sum_{j\in\mathcal T}(\hat x_j-x_j)^2}{|\mathcal T|}.
\end{equation}
Empty target support yields an unavailable value, not zero. Score B remains unavailable pending the pooled embedding, PCA rank, reference-bank size and overlap-exclusion design. Score C remains unavailable pending paired fitting-only transforms and a prospectively justified combination weight. No arbitrary defaults are reported as accepted results.

The diagnostic baselines include proportional-missing-branch Isolation Forest, a fixed missingness-aware feature map with a PSD RBF one-class SVM, and an observed-entry Inexact ALM RPCA variant with frozen projection. RPCA remains \texttt{NONCONVERGED\_MAX\_ITERATIONS} after 150 iterations. CUSUM chronology is unavailable because actual source-arrival times are not bound. Shared row identifiers do not make raw method scores commensurate or restore independent support.

POT/GPD and $k\ge2$ persistence primitives have numerical tests, but the pilot supplies no independent threshold-calibration set or verified lake-year exposure. The objective of one false alarm per lake-year is not achieved or estimated. The sequence primitive distinguishes sustained episode continuation from a new opening and declares at the sustaining arrival; it is not applied to create real alarms under an unavailable threshold.

\section{Validity, Reproducibility and Reporting Boundaries}
The diagnostic package is limited by sparse acquisitions, two development-exposed sites, unresolved physical sampling memberships, approximate SAR geometry and unknown historical availability. Seeds, spatial cells, tokens and repeated groups do not increase independent lake/event counts. Fitting provenance $G_P$ is separate from physical sampling graph $G_S$; shared model weights alone do not collapse distinct geographic sampling units.

The reporting milestone has 11 focused test passes, 423 core passes and 477 supporting software-verification passes, with one skip in each full suite. These 911 test passes are engineering checks, not 911 independent experiments or a scientific guarantee. Source/input/output hashes, actual replay arithmetic and retained adverse results support the stated diagnostic claims. They do not replace independent review or statistical identification.

No PR-AUC curve, operational warning threshold, control-lake non-event label or validated precursor result is asserted. Administrative inventory geometry is not a prerequisite for orbital extraction, but lake-specific quantitative claims still require an appropriately validated physical mask and registration support. A calendar cutoff alone does not supply historical product availability. Current outputs remain outside scientific admission and the project is not ready for submission.

\section{Conclusion}
The retained evidence supports source-bound engineering diagnostics, reproducible masked reconstruction on exposed support and genuine checkpoint-attention visualization. It does not yet answer whether Sentinel-GL improves GLOF monitoring or warning. The next scientific dependencies are an adequate temporal corpus, verified spatial and temporal support, independent event/non-event ascertainment, disjoint calibration/evaluation and the full planned comparative and sensitivity program. Unavailable and nonconverged results remain explicit rather than being replaced with favorable values.
